FAQs

Before placing an order.

Costs, location, funding, safety and working with local authorities.

How much does an arena in a park or square cost?

Panna Cage 90 starts at PLN 36,000 excluding VAT, or PLN 47,400 excluding VAT with concrete slab and painting. A 3v3 court (Street Arena 15) starts at PLN 100,000 excluding VAT. Installation, transport and site preparation are quoted after reviewing photos.

How much space is needed?

A 1v1 arena fits a site with sides of approximately 5 to 7 m. A panna court fits 30–60 m², a 3v3 court 120–250 m²; above 500 m² I recommend a bespoke design. Additional surrounding safety clearance is required.

Can you help with a participatory budget proposal?

Yes. The city package (Panna Cage 90 + Garden Plan pattern) includes materials for a participatory budget proposal: concept, visualisation and indicative price for the application form.

Where can a city obtain funding?

In Poland, options include Ministry of Sport programmes (including Orlik renovation), regional programmes, participatory budgets, village funds, European Funds for regeneration, or a partner funding the arena with branding on the boards. I prepare the technical section of the application.

Is an arena safe in a publicly accessible space?

Structures are steel and enclosed by boards; Panna Ring has stood at a publicly accessible Orlik facility for 10 years without damage. Surface standards: EPDM PN-EN 14877, rubber tiles PN-EN 1177 with PZH certification.

Can you help activate the space after opening?

Yes. I can organise an opening tournament with hosting and music and suggest recurring activities. I have organised tournaments since 2008 and organised the Freestival Street Games World Championships in 2017.

Can you renovate an old municipal court?

Yes. I assess the site from photos, repair the substrate and apply rubberised sports paint (from PLN 78/m² excluding VAT) or a new surface. If the substrate is damaged, I propose a new slab (PLN 283/m²).

How do you work with local authorities?

Commission an individual stage or the entire process: assessment, concept, design, delivery and opening. You receive technical data sheets and certificates for tender documentation and a 12-month warranty.
