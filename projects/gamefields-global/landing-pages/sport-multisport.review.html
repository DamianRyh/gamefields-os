Multi-sport design requires clear priorities.

A multi-use court should be intuitive. We establish the priority sports, how user groups will alternate and which equipment can share the same space. This prevents the court from becoming a jumble of coloured markings.

Frequently asked questions

One court, many possibilities.

Which sports can be combined on one court?

Football, basketball, volleyball, tennis and badminton are commonly combined, but the exact mix should reflect the dimensions and actual user needs.

Does a multi-use court need to be large?

No. Smaller multi-sport zones can also be designed if priorities, dimensions and equipment are selected appropriately.

How do you avoid cluttered markings?

We design a colour hierarchy and limit the number of equally prominent markings. Priority sports should be immediately recognisable.

Can another function be added later?

Often yes, if the structure and layout allow sufficient room. Future extensions are worth considering at the design stage.
