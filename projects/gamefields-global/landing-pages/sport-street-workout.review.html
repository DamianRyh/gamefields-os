Training in public spaces

Equipment is only part of the design.

A good street workout zone requires a logical station layout, safe surfacing, appropriate spacing and clear circulation. It works best as part of a wider activity space.

FAQ

How do you plan a street workout zone?

Can street workout be added to an existing court?

Yes. If the site allows, a training zone can complement an existing facility and broaden its uses.

Is safety surfacing required?

Surface selection depends on the specific equipment, free fall height and safety requirements.

Do you design the equipment layout?

Yes. The layout should reflect exercises, user circulation and required safety zones, not simply the available equipment catalogue.
