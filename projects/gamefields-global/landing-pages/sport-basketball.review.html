Half-court, 3x3 or full court?

We start by considering the site and users. A half-court or 3x3 court often works better on a compact site; larger areas can combine basketball with other sports and recreational zones.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions before building a basketball court.

Can a basketball court be built on a small site?

Yes. A half-court or 3x3 court provides a meaningful sporting function in a much smaller area than a conventional full court.

Which surface works well for basketball?

Selection depends on the substrate and intensity of use. We generally assess synthetic systems or suitably prepared hard surfaces.

Do you design colourful basketball courts?

Yes. Court design can include a complete colour composition, graphics and branding while keeping sports markings legible.
