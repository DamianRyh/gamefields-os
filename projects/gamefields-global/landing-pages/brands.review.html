FAQs

Sport as a brand platform.

Costs, formats, mobility, branding and timing.

How much does a sports brand activation cost?

It depends on the format. Mobile arena (rental and sale) from PLN 27,000 excluding VAT; a permanent branded 3v3 court structure from PLN 100,000 excluding VAT. The programme, hosting, music and content are quoted against the brief.

Which brands have you worked with?

These include Head & Shoulders (an event at Złote Tarasy in Warsaw, at a stadium in Gdańsk and a tournament with Legia Warszawa), and a UEFA Champions League event with a panna arena. I also organised the 2017 Freestival Street Games World Championships.

Can you deliver just the court without an event?

Yes. The scope can cover only design and delivery of a branded space or full production including a sports programme.

Do you offer mobile solutions?

Yes. A mobile panna arena uses modular boards and goals for temporary activations in shopping centres, festivals, stadiums or city squares. It can be rented or purchased.

Do you design formats from scratch?

Yes. I start with the marketing brief and propose the sport, participation mechanics, space and how to use the event in communications.

How large can the logo be on the court?

I design logos on the surface and boards so they do not compromise the clarity of play. Preview the pattern and brand colours in 3D Studio on your selected model.

Can a brand fund a permanent urban court?

Yes. This is one way to fund courts: a company funds an arena and its logo appears on the boards and surface. It works well at schools, in parks and residential estates.

How long does it take to prepare an activation?

It depends on the format and whether the arena is rented or newly manufactured. I confirm timing after the brief, before anything is signed.
