FAQ

Tennis court design and renewal.

Do you modernise existing courts?

Yes. First we assess the stability and condition of the existing layers to determine whether renovation is possible or more extensive reconstruction is needed.

How do you choose a tennis court surface?

Selection depends on the desired playing characteristics, intensity of use, maintenance and site conditions.

Can a court serve more than one purpose?

At school and recreational facilities, additional markings and equipment can be considered provided they do not compromise the clarity of the primary tennis function.
