FAQ

Organising football tournaments and sports events.

Planning a tournament for children, adults or company employees? Start with participants, location and playing time. Then we select the format, court and event production.

How do you organise a football tournament for children?

Specify participant ages, team numbers, playing level and event duration in the brief. We select the match format, breaks, rules and hosting style for the group. We also agree supervision, sports staffing and support facilities with the organiser. The 1v1 and 3v3 formats make use of compact spaces, but the design must also allow for waiting areas and spectators.

Do you organise football tournaments for adults and businesses?

We can deliver the sporting part of an event for employees, customers or a local community. We agree team numbers, participant ability, schedule, staffing and competition rules. The scope can include a mobile court, tournament coordination, referees, hosting, DJ and branding. Details are tailored to the brief and budget.

What information is needed for a tournament quotation?

Send the date, location, site photos, available dimensions, participant numbers and ages, and event duration. Tell us whether you need only a court or also competition organisation, sound, branding and photography. This lets us compare infrastructure-only delivery with full event production. Send your event brief.

Can a court be installed at a festival or in a brand zone?

We start by checking the substrate, available area, access and installation conditions. The layout includes the playing area, entrances, spectator zone and supporting facilities. See mobile court rental and sports activations for brands.

Can you rent just the court?

Yes. If you handle event production yourself, we can supply the selected court system and agree the transport and installation scope.

Do you produce events for businesses and brands?

Yes. The format can be aimed at employees, customers, a local community or participants in a larger festival or campaign.

Does the event have to involve football?

No. Gamefields also develops formats involving basketball and other urban sports, depending on the site and brief.
