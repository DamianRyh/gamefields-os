    FAQs

    Turf, EPDM or paint?

    Common questions about selection, prices, substrates and durability.

    Which surface is best for a court?

    There is no single best surface. Football typically uses artificial turf or EPDM; basketball, volleyball and multi-use courts use EPDM or acrylic; rubber tiles or mats suit areas requiring impact attenuation; and sports coatings can refresh old concrete. I start with the sport, substrate and intended use.

    How much does a square metre of sports surfacing cost?

    From PLN 78/m² (painting) to PLN 492/m² (Premium rubber tiles), excluding VAT. A concrete slab with sub-base, where required, costs PLN 283/m².

    What is a synthetic surface?

    A group of systems made from synthetic materials. For courts, the term usually refers to polyurethane (EPDM) or acrylic systems; artificial turf is compared separately. Systems differ in elasticity, installation, substrate requirements and maintenance.

    Can a surface be installed on existing concrete?

    Often yes, if the substrate is stable and in good condition. Assessment and surface preparation are necessary. Substrate preparation (milling, crack repair) is quoted separately after reviewing photos.

    Do you install the sub-base?

    Yes. A 12 cm concrete slab (C20/25) with gradients and movement joints, on geotextile and an aggregate sub-base, with reinforcement mesh: PLN 283/m² excluding VAT.

    Can I see samples?

    Yes. I can send surface samples by post. Tell me which surfaces to compare and where to send them.

    How long will the surface last?

    It depends on the system and option. For EPDM: Standard 8–12 years, Standard+ 10–15 years, Premium 12–20 years. Premium PP tiles have a 5–10-year warranty. I provide a 12-month warranty on the complete court and support after handover.

    Can the surface graphics be customised?

    Yes. Colours, zones, patterns and markings are part of the design. Change the surface and pattern on your selected court in 3D Studio and see the price immediately.
