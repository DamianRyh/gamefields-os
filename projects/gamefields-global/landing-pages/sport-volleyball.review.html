FAQ

What should you establish before building a volleyball court?

Can volleyball be combined with other sports?

Yes. It is a common feature of multi-use courts, especially at schools and recreational spaces.

How do you choose a surface?

Consider the location, frequency of use, other sports and maintenance requirements.

Can the posts be removable?

Yes. At multi-use facilities, a removable system allows faster changes between activities.
