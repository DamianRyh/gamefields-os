FAQs

Rental and tournaments.

Costs, duration, indoor venues, branding, tournaments for children and companies.

How much does mobile court rental cost?

Rental is quoted for specific dates and venues, depending on duration, transport, installation, branding and staffing. A mobile arena can also be purchased: from PLN 27,000 excluding VAT (PLN 33,210 including VAT) for the structure.

How long can a court be rented for?

The scope is agreed per project, from a single event to an extended temporary activation. For multiple dates in a season, compare rental and purchase.

Can the court be installed indoors?

Yes, an arena was installed indoors for the Head & Shoulders Freestyle Football Cup 2013. Suitability depends on the system, substrate, dimensions and venue conditions, so I check the location and installation access before quoting.

Can sponsor branding be added?

Yes. Branding can cover boards, equipment and surrounding staging. Options depend on the court model and components.

Do you organise the whole tournament or just deliver the court?

Either. I can deliver the court with transport and installation or prepare the entire sports element of the event: format, referees, hosting, DJ and branding.

How do you organise a football tournament for children?

Specify participant ages, number of teams, playing level and event duration in the brief. I then select the match format, breaks, rules and hosting. 1v1 and 3v3 formats make good use of small spaces, but waiting areas and spectators must also be planned.

Do you organise tournaments for companies and employees?

Yes. I agree the number of teams, participant level, schedule, staffing and competition rules. The scope can include a mobile court, coordination, referees, hosting, DJ and branding.

What is needed for a quote?

Date, location, venue photos, available dimensions, participant numbers and ages, and duration. Include whether you need only the court or also competition organisation, sound, branding and photography.
