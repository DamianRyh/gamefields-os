Participatory budget · Warsaw

Terrace by the Palace of Culture.

I co-initiated a project funded through Warsaw's participatory budget: courts at the Terrace in Świętokrzyski Park, beside the Palace of Culture and Science. Delivered in cooperation with Warsaw Greenery Authority, the project shows how a small arena can activate public space in the heart of a city.

2025 – 2026

What I am building now.

Three projects, one idea: a good place to play and regular activity build a community.

GF · 01

Gamefields

Street football court concepts, renovation and construction. Modular boards, 1v1 and 3v3 zones, brand activations. Quotations, cost estimates and 3D visualisations for local authorities, investors and partners.

Open 3D Studio

GF · 02

City Football Concept

A model for scaling local football communities. Warsaw FC's experience translated into spaces, regular activity, events, partners and communications, ready to transfer to other cities.

GF · 03

Polish Football Club

A platform concept connecting football communities, leagues, partners and events, with tools for developing football projects.
