Construction step by step

How an arena is built.

Wake For Friends wakeboarding park in Grodzisk Mazowiecki. At the start: sand, water nearby and no prepared base. At the end: a Panna Octa Cage 180 arena used for everyday play and park events. Here is what happened in between.

  1. 01

    Site

    We assess the site: the ground conditions, drainage and where players will enter. Here there was only sand, so the arena needed its own stable base.

  2. 02

    Platform

    We lay a platform of pallets and boards on the sand. It distributes the weight of the structure and provides a level, dry base for the playing surface.

  3. 03

    Level platform

    We join the boards into one continuous plane. Its evenness determines how the ball rolls.

  4. 04

    Surface underlay

    We lay an underlay on the platform so the turf sits evenly across the entire base.

  5. 05

    Surface

    Artificial turf across the entire platform, with an allowance around the arena.

  6. 06

    Structure and play

    We install the frame modules and goals. The arena serves everyday play and park events, including children's birthday parties, stag parties and day camps.

On a hard base.

On concrete or paving, a platform is not needed. The arena then stands on a prepared base, and we select the surface to suit the site.
