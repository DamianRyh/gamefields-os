Questions

Before you choose an arena.

Prices, differences between games, space, durability and rental.

How much does a panna court cost?

Structures range from PLN 36,000 excluding VAT (Panna Cage 90) to PLN 100,000 excluding VAT (Street Arena 15). With a concrete slab and painting, Panna Play, for example, starts at PLN 51,000 excluding VAT. Installation, transport and site preparation are quoted after reviewing site photos.

What is included in the structure price?

The structure with goals in the basic configuration and standard colour. Surfacing, installation, transport and graphics are excluded. The price with surface adds a 12 cm concrete slab with sub-base and single-colour painting.

How does panna differ from 3v3 street football?

Panna is 1v1 play in a small enclosed arena: technique and playing the ball between an opponent's legs matter. 3v3 street football is fast team play on a larger boarded court, represented in our range by the 15 × 10 m Street Arena 15.

How much space does a panna court need?

The smallest arenas are approximately 5 m in diameter, and the surface area with a 10% allowance starts at 21.5 m² (Panna Ring). A 3v3 court measures 15 × 10 m. Additional safety space is needed around it. Check your site dimensions in the “Will it fit?” planner.

Which arena is best for a school or residential community?

For schools and residential communities I usually recommend Panna Cage 90 (low and visually open) and Panna Play; for municipalities and parks, Panna Octa; for clubs and academies, Panna Cage 180 with a higher enclosure. The final choice depends on the site and age group.

Can I see the model before ordering?

Yes. View each model in 3D, then choose the frame colour, surface and pattern in Studio and see the price immediately. Download the design as a PDF project sheet.

How long will the arena last?

Panna Ring has stood at the OSiR Orlik facility in Grodzisk Mazowiecki for 10 years without damage. I provide a 12-month warranty and ongoing support after handover.

Can an arena be rented for an event?

Yes. Mobile event arenas are available for rental or purchase. Purchase starts at PLN 27,000 excluding VAT; rental is quoted separately. They have been used at tournaments, championships and brand activations.
