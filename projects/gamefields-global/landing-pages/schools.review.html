Funding

Six funding sources in Poland.

Each has different thresholds and requirements. Start with a location and an idea; I can suggest which might suit your site.

  • Polish Ministry of Sport and Tourism

    Programmes for new and renovated facilities, including “Moje Boisko – Orlik 2012” and regional infrastructure development.

    For: municipalities, counties and schools through their governing bodies
  • Regional government

    Polish regional authorities run their own programmes and co-fund ministry programmes, such as “Sportowa Małopolska”.

    For: municipalities and counties in the region
  • Participatory budget

    A potential route for a single arena in a park or at school.

    For: residents, neighbourhood councils and schools
  • Village fund

    A Polish village community may allocate funds to creating a court. A small arena can suit the scale of this fund.

    For: village communities in rural municipalities
  • European Funds

    Regional programmes for 2021–2027 fund regeneration. A court may form part of a site or neighbourhood renewal project.

    For: municipalities with a regeneration programme
  • Brand or foundation

    A company funds the arena and its logo appears on the boards and surface.

    For: schools, clubs, residential estates and companies

FAQs

Applications, prices, documentation.

Frequently asked questions from schools, municipalities and sports centres.

Who submits the funding application?

An eligible organisation, usually a municipality, county or school through its governing body. I prepare the technical section: concept, visualisation, description and indicative price for the cost estimate.

When should preparation for 2027 funding rounds begin?

Ideally several weeks before applications open. Poland's Ministry of Sport announced the first 2027 rounds from 4 January 2027, with “Moje Boisko – Orlik 2012” applications until 26 February 2027. Aim to have the concept and estimate ready in December.

How much own contribution is required?

In Poland's 2026 regional sports infrastructure development programme, applicants covered at least 40% of eligible costs, with funding of up to 60%. Check current rules for future editions.

Can a small panna court receive funding?

It depends on the programme. Consider including a small arena in a larger project, such as an Orlik renovation or a school sports zone. Smaller amounts may be easier to fund through participatory budgets, village funds or regional programmes.

How much does a school arena cost?

Panna Cage 90 from PLN 36,000 excluding VAT (with slab and painting from PLN 47,400), Panna Play from PLN 40,000 excluding VAT (with slab and painting from PLN 51,000). Installation, transport and site preparation are quoted after reviewing photos.

Can you renovate an existing Orlik or school court?

Yes. I assess the substrate and equipment from photos, then propose renovation or reconstruction. Solutions meet the requirements of Poland's “Moje Boisko – Orlik 2012” programme; Panna Ring has stood at the Orlik in Grodzisk Mazowiecki for 10 years.

Will I receive tender documentation?

Yes. I include technical data sheets and certificates for steel, paint and surfaces in quotations, applications and tender documentation. EPDM surfaces meet PN-EN 14877; rubber tiles meet PN-EN 1177 with PZH certification.

Can you help with a participatory budget proposal?

Yes. I prepare a concept, visualisation and indicative price for the submission form.

Can a small school courtyard be used?

Yes. 1v1 arenas start at approximately 5 m in diameter, and a 3v3 court measures 15 × 10 m. Check courtyard dimensions in the “Will it fit?” planner on the homepage.
