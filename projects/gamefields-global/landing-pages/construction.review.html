For a quote

What should you send me?

No documentation or technical expertise is needed. Five details and a few phone photos are enough to start.

Five details

  • Location: address or map pin.
  • Dimensions: approximate, measured or taken from a map.
  • Sports and users: who will play and which sports.
  • Scope: complete court, surfacing only or renovation.
  • Timing and budget: including any funding-related constraints.

Site photos

  • The whole surface from several angles.
  • Close-ups of cracks, defects and edges.
  • Drains and areas of standing water.
  • Existing equipment and foundations.

FAQs

Before enquiring.

Common questions about costs, scope, funding and timing.

How much does sports court construction cost?

Gamefields structures start at PLN 36,000 excluding VAT, and a complete court with concrete slab and painting, such as Panna Play, from PLN 51,000 excluding VAT. A concrete slab with sub-base costs PLN 283/m², and finishes range from PLN 78 to 492/m² excluding VAT. Installation, transport and site preparation are quoted after reviewing site photos.

What does the “with surface” price include?

Structure in basic configuration and standard colour, a 12 cm concrete slab with sub-base and single-colour painting, calculated from the structure footprint plus 10%. Excludes installation, transport and site preparation.

Do you also build larger multi-use courts?

Yes, as a bespoke project: site assessment, concept, sub-base, surface, markings and equipment. The price is quoted after reviewing photos, dimensions and intended sports, as it depends on the scope of work.

Can a court be built on old concrete?

Usually, yes. If the concrete is stable, I repair cracks and defects, prime the substrate and apply a flexible sports coating with rubber granules, then graphics and markings. Severely damaged substrates must first be rebuilt.

How much space do I need?

The smallest arenas are approximately 5 m in diameter; a 3v3 court measures 15 × 10 m. Additional safety clearance must be allowed. Check site dimensions in the “Will it fit?” planner on the homepage.

Can I see the design and price before ordering?

Yes. Choose the model, colours, pattern and surface in 3D Studio, with live pricing. Download the project PDF and submit it for a quotation.

Can you help with funding and tenders?

Yes. For municipalities and schools, I prepare the concept, technical specifications and funding and tender documentation, including manufacturers' data sheets and certificates. Polish 2027 funding rounds open from 4 January.

What warranty is provided?

A 12-month warranty and ongoing renovation support after handover. Panna Ring at the Grodzisk Mazowiecki municipal sports centre's Orlik facility has been in use for 10 years without damage.

When can work be carried out?

Sports coatings are applied to dry concrete at temperatures above freezing, usually from spring to autumn. Design, quotation and documentation can be prepared earlier, including winter.
