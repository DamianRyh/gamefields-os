Gamefields Studio

Concepts.

Places that could still come to life: the square by the Palace of Culture, a court under Łazienkowski Bridge, old sites in need of renewal. I start with a site photo and a conversation about who will play there. Then I draw a court that fits the city, rather than picking one from a catalogue.

Square by the Palace of Culture.

A concept for the square by the Palace of Culture and Science featuring Gamefields courts: playing areas integrated into the paving, alongside spaces for riding and relaxing.

Łazienkowski Bridge.

A site beneath the bridge structure, near the riverside boulevards. The concept introduces a court with a clear surface pattern where there is currently only empty concrete.
