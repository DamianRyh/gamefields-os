Renovation or new court construction?

When should you renovate an old court or build a new one? Check the sub-base, drainage, surface, equipment and intended use. Finally, see how I turn old concrete into a colourful court.

Short answer

If the substrate is stable and issues mainly concern surfacing, markings and equipment, renovation is usually sufficient. At Gamefields, sports painting of an existing site starts at PLN 78/m² excluding VAT.

When does renovation make sense?

  • the substrate has no extensive settlement or active cracks,
  • drainage works or can be improved without complete demolition,
  • dimensions and location still suit users,
  • most issues concern surfacing, markings and equipment,
  • the new function can use the existing geometry.

When should you consider rebuilding?

  • the sub-base is unstable and causes permanent deformation,
  • the facility has serious drainage problems,
  • existing dimensions cannot accommodate the required functions,
  • the entire surface and underlying structure need replacement,
  • planned infrastructure requires new foundations and site reconstruction.

In that case, Gamefields starts with a 12 cm concrete slab with sub-base (PLN 283/m² excluding VAT), on which the selected finish is installed.

The best option is often a middle ground

Renewal need not mean simply repainting an old court. Parts of the structure can be retained, the substrate repaired locally, a new wearing layer installed, equipment added and the visual character transformed. This scope should follow inspection rather than assumptions.

Old concrete, new court

  1. Site assessment

    Using photos and dimensions, I assess cracks, falls, drainage and concrete condition. I explain whether painting is enough or the substrate needs repair.

  2. Repair and priming

    Cleaning, grinding, filling cracks and defects, concrete primer and levelling layer. Colour durability depends on these steps.

  3. Rubber-modified paints

    Flexible sports coating with rubber granules: cushions steps, provides slip resistance and covers minor cracks. For higher requirements, polyurethane spray with EPDM.

  4. Pattern and markings

    Signature pattern or your Studio 3D design. Markings for football, basketball, 3v3 or multiple games.

Painting prices

  • Single colour: Primer, 2 coats of sports paint and standard markings. PLN 78/m² excluding VAT.
  • Single colour with granules: Flexible rubber-modified coating: improved cushioning and slip resistance, with markings. PLN 100/m² excluding VAT.
  • Intermediate pattern: 4–6 colours, Signature-style geometric patterns, on a granulated base. PLN 150/m² excluding VAT.
  • Advanced pattern: 7 or more colours, complex graphics (Organic Flow, gradients), on a granulated base. PLN 183/m² excluding VAT.

Sports paints are applied to dry concrete above freezing, usually from spring to autumn. I quote substrate repairs after inspection.

What should you photograph before a consultation?

Photograph the entire surface, cracks and defects, edges, drains, standing water, equipment foundations and existing equipment. Dimensions and location are also useful.
