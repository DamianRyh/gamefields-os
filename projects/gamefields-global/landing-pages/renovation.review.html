Decision

Renovate or build a new court?

If the substrate is stable and the issues mainly concern the surface, markings and equipment, renovation is usually sufficient. Full guide →

Renovation makes sense when

  • the substrate has no extensive settlement or moving cracks,
  • drainage works or can be improved without complete demolition,
  • the dimensions and location still suit users,
  • most issues concern the surface, markings and equipment,
  • the new function can use the existing geometry.

Painting from PLN 78/m² or new surfacing from PLN 167/m² excluding VAT. Calculate ↓

Rebuilding makes sense when

  • the sub-base is unstable and causes permanent deformation,
  • the facility has serious drainage problems,
  • existing dimensions cannot accommodate the required functions,
  • the surface and underlying structure both need replacement,
  • new infrastructure requires foundations and site reconstruction.

New 12 cm concrete slab with sub-base: PLN 283/m² excluding VAT. Court construction →

Most projects fall somewhere in between: I retain part of the site, repair the substrate locally, apply a new layer, add equipment and transform the character of the space. The scope follows inspection rather than a predetermined assumption.

FAQs

Renovation instead of demolition.

Costs, scope, timing and funding.

How much does court renovation cost?

Sports coating costs PLN 78–183/m² excluding VAT, depending on the option, from one colour with markings to a pattern with 7+ colours. New surfacing on an existing site starts at PLN 167/m² excluding VAT. Substrate repairs (cracks, grinding, levelling) are quoted after reviewing photos.

Can every old court be renovated?

Not always. Extensive settlement, moving cracks or serious drainage issues require rebuilding the substrate. The first step is therefore assessing the site from photos and dimensions.

Do you renovate concrete and asphalt courts?

Yes. I clean and repair stable concrete or asphalt and use it as a base for painting or new surfacing.

How does a granulated coating differ from ordinary paint?

The granulated option is a flexible rubberised coating: it provides better cushioning, slip resistance and covers fine cracks. I also use it for multicolour patterns.

Can the court layout change during renovation?

Yes. New markings for football, basketball, 3v3 or multiple games, different play zones, new graphics, or even a panna arena with boards.

How long does renovation take?

It depends on court size and the initial site condition. I confirm the schedule after reviewing photos, before anything is signed.

When can a court be painted?

Sports coatings are applied to dry concrete at temperatures above freezing, usually from spring to autumn. Assessment, design and quotation can take place earlier, including winter.

Can I preview the new appearance before renovation?

Yes. Design patterns, colours and markings in 3D Studio with live pricing. This is how the Organic Flow concept for Pole Mokotowskie was created.

Can you help with an Orlik renovation?

Yes. I prepare the concept, specifications, funding and tender documentation, including manufacturers' data sheets and certificates. Polish 2027 funding rounds open from 4 January.
