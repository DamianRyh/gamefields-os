Paintable, not just attractive

A pattern that can actually be painted.

The on-screen design must transfer to concrete without compromising quality or play. That is why Studio patterns follow rules.

  • ~20 cmNarrowest band

    Each band, cell and outline in Masters II is at least approximately 20 cm on a 15 × 10 m court.

  • ≤ 5Paint colours per pattern

    No more than five paints from the palette. Overprint adds one mixed colour at intersections.

  • 1 : 1Symmetry

    Composition symmetrical about the centre of the court, so both halves play the same.

  • 0On-screen gradients

    Flat shapes only. Gradients become stepped bands that can be painted.

Questions

Court design that remains a sports court.

Costs, substrate, patterns, logos and markings.

How much does court painting cost?

From PLN 78/m² (one colour, primer, 2 paint coats and markings) to PLN 183/m² (7+ colour pattern on a granulated base), excluding VAT. For a 28 × 15 m court (420 m²), prices start at PLN 32,760 excluding VAT. Substrate repairs are quoted after reviewing photos.

Can an existing concrete or asphalt court be painted?

Yes, provided the surface is stable and properly prepared: cleaning, grinding, crack filling, primer and levelling layer. I assess substrate condition from photos before selecting the technology.

Do you design patterns or only paint them?

Both. You can design your own pattern in Studio 3D (732 patterns, each with 12 variants and palettes), I can create a bespoke design or implement an approved visual identity.

Can complex patterns be painted accurately?

Studio patterns are designed for painting: flat shapes only, with gradients represented by stepped bands. In Masters II each band is at least approximately 20 cm on a 15 × 10 m court, the pattern uses no more than 5 paints and is symmetrical, so both halves play the same.

Can logos be placed on the court?

Yes. Branding can appear on the playing area, boards, zones outside the court or equipment. I design it to preserve clarity of play.

Do you provide sports markings alone?

Yes. Markings for football, basketball, 3v3 or multiple games, without necessarily changing the overall appearance. I select the marking colour (white, black or lime) for the best contrast with the surface.

How does rubber-granulated paint differ from standard paint?

It is a flexible rubber-modified coating providing better cushioning, slip resistance and coverage of minor cracks. I apply multicolour patterns on this base.

When can painting be carried out?

On dry concrete above freezing, usually from spring to autumn. Design and quotations can be prepared earlier, including in winter.
