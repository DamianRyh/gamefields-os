How much does it cost to build a sports court?

Court costs depend on area, sub-base, surfacing, equipment and surrounding infrastructure. Rather than a single price, break the investment down into the elements that actually make up the budget. Gamefields prices are also shown below.

Short answer

There is no single price for a court: two similarly sized facilities can cost very different amounts if one uses an existing sub-base and the other requires earthworks, drainage, fencing and lighting. Gamefields starting prices: structure from PLN 36,000 excluding VAT (Panna Octa Cage 90); complete court with slab and painting, e.g. Panna Play, from PLN 51,000 excluding VAT.

What has the greatest impact on court costs?

01Site condition
The biggest difference is between installation on an existing stable surface and a project requiring excavation, earthworks and a complete sub-base.
02Area
Area affects material and labour requirements, but not all costs increase linearly. A small court still needs design, transport and equipment.
03Surface
Artificial turf, polyurethane, modular systems and prepared hard surfaces differ in construction, installation costs and maintenance.
04Equipment
Goals, hoops, nets, rebound boards, ball-stop nets, fencing, benches and lighting can account for a significant portion of the investment.
05Logistics
Transport, heavy equipment access, foundations and installation depend on location and site conditions.

What is included and what is quoted separately?

The Gamefields concrete slab includes:

  • Excavation and ground preparation
  • Geotextile and aggregate sub-base
  • 12 cm concrete slab (C20/25) with falls and expansion joints
  • Reinforcement mesh
  • Surface ready for finishing: paint, tiles, turf or EPDM
  • Area allowance of 10% relative to the structure

Quoted separately after reviewing site photos:

  • Complex drainage or soil replacement deeper than 30 cm
  • Restricted access and additional logistics
  • Structure installation and transport

Why can online prices be misleading?

The phrase “court construction cost” often compares very different investments. A surface layer alone is not the same as a complete court. Public procurement in 2026 includes multi-use projects worth hundreds of thousands of PLN, but their scope includes earthworks, drainage, surfacing, fencing and site furniture. Such amounts should not be treated as a price list for every project.

How to prepare information for an initial quotation?

For an initial budget we need five pieces of information: location, approximate dimensions, photos of the existing site, sports or intended use, and expected scope. If you do not know which surface technology to choose, that is exactly what the concept stage is for.

The cheapest option does not always mean the lowest overall investment cost

Consider durability, repairability, maintenance costs and future changes of use. A well-designed sub-base and sensible equipment can reduce later remedial work.
