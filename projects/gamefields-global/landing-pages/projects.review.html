Warsaw, Świętokrzyski Park

Terrace in Świętokrzyski Park

A multi-use recreation space for all ages: three courts connected by a terrace and skateboard ramp, beside the metro interchange near the Palace of Culture and Science.

The Terrace became one of the most popular meeting places for Warsaw's young people. Following the outbreak of war in Ukraine, it also became an important gathering place for Ukrainian youth, as reported by publications including The New York Times. It is a temporary installation anticipating further changes around the Palace of Culture and Science.
