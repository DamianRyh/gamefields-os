We select the format according to the site and style of play.

Compact play often suits schools, residential communities or urban squares better than a conventional large facility. Gamefields draws on street football experience to design spaces that support intensive play, are easy to understand and readily activated.

We then assess smaller formats: 1v1, 3v3 or a bespoke training zone layout.
