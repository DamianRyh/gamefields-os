Getting started

Have a site? That is enough.

You do not need a design or technical expertise. Show me the site and I will turn it into a specific court layout.

FAQs

Before we begin.

What to prepare, what you receive, costs and next steps.

What do I need to start a design?

Location, approximate dimensions, site photos and details of who will play and which sports. I prepare an initial concept from these. Documentation is not needed at the start.

Can I design a court myself?

Yes. In 3D Studio, choose the model, frame colour, surface and pattern, and the price updates live. Download the project PDF and submit it for a quote. Check site dimensions first in the “Will it fit?” planner.

What does a concept design include?

Court layout with zones and access, 3D view, pattern and markings, surface and substrate selection, and price. For municipalities and schools, technical specifications and funding and tender documents are also included.

How much does a designed court cost?

Gamefields structures start at PLN 36,000 excluding VAT, concrete slab with sub-base at PLN 283/m², and surfaces from PLN 78/m². 3D Studio displays the design price; installation, transport and site preparation are quoted after reviewing photos.

Can branding be included in the design?

Yes. City, school, club or partner colours and logos can be integrated into the surface, boards and staging without compromising clarity of play.

Can you help with a design for a funding application?

Yes. I prepare the concept, specifications and funding and tender documentation, including manufacturers' data sheets and certificates. Polish 2027 funding rounds open from 4 January.

Can you build the designed court afterwards?

Yes. Design can be a standalone stage or the start of construction, renovation or painting. That was the process in Kraków, from site plan to completed park arena.

How does a concept design differ from a visualisation?

A visualisation shows appearance. A Gamefields concept also includes dimensions, zones, surface, substrate and price: a solution that can be built. I always label visualisations and completed projects separately on the website.
