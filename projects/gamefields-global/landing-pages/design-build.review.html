Single point of responsibility

Design and construction should work towards the same outcome.

Under the design-and-build model, design decisions account for construction, budget and site feasibility from the outset. This helps identify conflicts between the concept, surfacing technology and equipment at an early stage.

FAQ

Design and build in sports infrastructure projects.

Can Gamefields handle both design and construction?

Yes, within the scope agreed for each project. Where specialist studies or professional qualifications are required, the work is coordinated with relevant specialists.

Is this approach suitable for local authorities?

It can be used for public projects, but the procurement method and formal requirements depend on the tender documentation. Gamefields prepares the technical and construction scope appropriate to the particular project.

Can we start without a completed design?

Yes. We can start with the location, user needs and planned budget, then define the solution before moving into construction.
