Six funding sources.

Each has different thresholds and requirements. A location and an idea are enough to start, and I can suggest which suits your site.

  • 01Ministry of Sport and TourismProgrammes for new and renewed facilities, including “Moje Boisko – Orlik 2012” and regional infrastructure development. In 2026, funding covered up to 60% of eligible costs.For municipalities, counties and schools through their governing bodies
  • 02RegionRegional authorities run their own programmes and co-finance ministerial programmes, such as “Sportowa Małopolska”.For municipalities and counties in the region
  • 03Participatory budgetingResidents submit projects, the authority assesses them, and residents vote. A suitable route for a single arena in a park or by a school.For residents, neighbourhood councils and schools
  • 04Village fundA village administrative unit can allocate its funds to facilities such as a sports court. A small arena is on a scale suited to this fund.For village administrative units in rural municipalities
  • 05European FundsRegional programmes for 2021–2027 fund regeneration projects. A court and colourful surfacing can form part of the renewal of a square or residential community.For municipalities with a regeneration programme
  • 06Brand or foundationA court with partner branding: a company funds the arena and its logo appears on the boards and surface.For schools, clubs, residential communities and businesses

Short answers.

Not sure where to start? Get in touch, and I will reply personally.

Who submits the funding application?

An eligible entity submits the application, usually a municipality, county or school through its governing body. Gamefields prepares the technical part: concept, visualisation, solution description and indicative price for the cost estimate.

Can a small panna court receive funding?

It depends on the programme. Ministerial programmes have their own thresholds and requirements, so a small arena may be worth including in a larger project, such as renewal of an Orlik facility or a school sports zone. Smaller amounts are easier to fund through participatory budgets, village funds or regional programmes.

How much own contribution is required?

In the 2026 edition of the regional sports infrastructure development programme, applicants covered at least 40% of eligible costs, with funding of up to 60%. We check current programme rules for future editions.

When should preparations begin?

Ideally several weeks before applications open. The Ministry of Sport announced the first 2027 application windows from 4 January 2027, so it is worth having the concept and cost estimate ready in December.

Do you help with participatory budgeting?

Yes. We prepare the concept, visualisation and indicative price for the form.
