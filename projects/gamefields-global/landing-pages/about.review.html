Gamefields / About us

We design places where people want to play.

Gamefields combines design, sports infrastructure construction, street sport and experience in community engagement. We see a court not just as surfacing and equipment, but as a place that needs to thrive after opening.

Our approach

From empty space to an active place.

We start with the location, users and problem to solve. Only then do we select the sport, scale, surfacing, equipment and technology. We deliver new courts and renew existing facilities.

01We designConcept, geometry, functions, surfacing, equipment, visual design and preparation for construction.
02We build and renovateSub-base, surfacing, structures, installation, renovation, painting and supporting infrastructure.
03We activateTournaments, opening events, sports programmes and activations that help turn a facility into a lively space.
Who we work with

City. School. Club. Residential community. Brand.

The same square metre can serve different purposes depending on the project owner. Rather than selling a single catalogue product, we build solutions around specific users and locations.

Cities and local authoritiesRegeneration and new public sports spaces.
SchoolsDurable courts for intensive daily use.
Property developersCompact sports amenities for residential developments.
Clubs and academiesAdditional training and small-sided playing zones.
BrandsBranded courts, pop-ups and sports activations.
