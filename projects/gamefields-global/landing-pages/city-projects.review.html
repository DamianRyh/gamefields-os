Why a panna arena.

Reasons we hear from clients.

  • Small footprintThe arena fits on a site approximately 5 to 7 m across, alongside existing courts.
  • DurabilityThe Panna Ring at the Orlik facility in Grodzisk Mazowiecki has stood undamaged for 10 years.
  • Informal playAll it takes is two people and a ball. No registration, coach or booking.
  • A distinctive landmarkThe colourful structure and surface attract attention and give the site a clear layout.

Grodzisk Mazowiecki, Orlik

Arena at an Orlik facility

Challenge

An additional playing zone at an existing sports facility for one-on-one and small-group play.

Solution

A Panna Ring arena at the Orlik facility: a compact enclosed space for 1v1 play.

Outcome

The arena has stood at the Orlik facility for 10 years and has served its purpose throughout that period without damage.

Solution

An octagonal Panna Octa arena on the promenade, commissioned by Kutno City Hall.

Warsaw, Świętokrzyski Park

Terrace by the Palace of Culture

Challenge

A multi-use recreational space in the city centre, near the intersection of metro lines and the Palace of Culture. A temporary development.

Solution

Three Panna Ring arenas connected to a terrace and a skateboarding ramp. Design: Michał Sikorski, Marcin Kwietowicz. Client: Warsaw Municipal Greenery Authority.

Outcome

The terrace became one of the most popular meeting places for young people in Warsaw. After the outbreak of war in Ukraine, it also became an important meeting place for Ukrainian youth, as reported by outlets including The New York Times.
