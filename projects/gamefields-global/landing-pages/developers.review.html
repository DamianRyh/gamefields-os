FAQs

Sport in residential developments.

Space, costs, design, neighbours and scheduling.

How much space does a residential court need?

A 1v1 arena fits on a site with sides of 5–7 m; the smallest surface including allowance is 21.5 m² (Panna Ring). A 3v3 court measures 15 × 10 m. Additional surrounding safety clearance is required.

How much does a residential sports zone cost?

Structures from PLN 36,000 excluding VAT. The residential package (Panna Court) starts at PLN 69,000 excluding VAT for the structure, or PLN 84,700 with slab and painting. Installation and transport are quoted after reviewing site photos.

Can the colours match the development's architecture?

Yes. In 3D Studio you can change frame and panel colours and the surface pattern: 732 patterns in 12 variants, with palettes. I can also create a pattern in the development's brand colours.

Can disruption to residents be reduced?

I select the location, game type and boards with nearby homes in mind during concept design. Options include mesh, perforated steel, bars or solid panels.

Can you prepare residential sales materials?

Yes. Studio renders in the development's colours, a site-photo visualisation, a project PDF and a short amenity description for the brochure.

When should the arena be installed?

During landscaping, once access routes and substrate are ready. Paint and surfaces are applied to dry substrates above freezing, usually from spring to autumn.

Who will look after the court after handover?

I provide a 12-month warranty on delivery and ongoing surface support. After years of use, the paint can be refreshed without replacing the structure.

Is the arena durable in a publicly accessible space?

Panna Ring has stood at the publicly accessible Orlik facility in Grodzisk Mazowiecki for 10 years without damage. Structures are made of steel, with manufacturers' certificates.
