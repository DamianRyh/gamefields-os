Before getting in touch.

Short answers to common questions.

Do I need a completed design?

No. A site is enough: address or map pin, approximate dimensions and a photo. Start designing in 3D Studio or leave it to me.

We have no budget. Is it worth suggesting a site?

Yes. Select “I want to suggest a site”. In Poland, many courts are funded through participatory budgets, school or municipal funds, or brand sponsorship. Your proposal and design are a good starting point.

I am under 16. What should I do?

Submit your enquiry with a parent, guardian or teacher and provide their email address. I will reply to both of you.

How much does a court cost?

Structures from PLN 36,000 excluding VAT; a complete court with slab and painting, such as Panna Play, from PLN 51,000 excluding VAT. All prices are available in the shop and 3D Studio.
