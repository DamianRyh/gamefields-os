This notice describes contact through gamefields.eu and use of the Gamefields Studio configurator. It explains what data you provide, what your browser stores and how to exercise your rights.

Who is responsible for your data and how to contact us

Damian Gawrych, who operates Damian Gawrych's studio, is responsible for data processing within the studio. For privacy matters, email gawrych.damian@gmail.com or call 794 911 746. These contact details can also be used to submit data rights requests.

Contact and preparing quotations

On participatory budgeting campaign pages, we store an email address only if the organiser requests help with the application; it is used solely for contact about that matter.

The contact form requires an email address. You may also provide your name or organisation name, city or project site, message, telephone number, project type and approximate dimensions. Providing data is voluntary. Without contact details, we cannot respond to your enquiry.

Enquiries submitted through the form are directed to info@gamefields.eu. Your email address is used to reply to your message.

General correspondence is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding and conducting business — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Preparing a quotation at your request, where you are to be a party to the contract, is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. When you contact us on behalf of an organisation, the basis is our legitimate interest in managing that relationship. Data needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims may be processed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to a newsletter. Please do not send health data, document identification numbers or other information unnecessary for discussing the project.

How Studio saves your project

Studio saves a draft locally in your browser under the key gamefields-studio-draft-v1. The draft may include the project name, court settings, lettering and uploaded logos. This lets you resume work on the same device and in the same browser.

Uploading a PNG or SVG logo and importing a project are handled in your browser. The logo becomes part of the project and local draft. Downloading JSON saves a copy of the project to your device. Do not include confidential information or personal data that is unnecessary for preparing the project.

Moving from Studio to an enquiry transfers a text summary to the form on gamefields.eu. You can review and remove attached specifications before submission. Opening the form with specifications does not itself send an enquiry.

You can delete the local draft by clearing Studio site data in your browser settings. Delete downloaded JSON copies separately from your device. Deleting the draft does not delete correspondence previously sent to the studio.

Visitor analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand which parts of the website and Studio help with court design. Analytics is disabled without consent.

After you give consent, Google Analytics stores the cookies _ga and _ga_* and collects visited pages, visit duration, device and browser type, approximate location (country and city), and traffic source. We do not combine this data with enquiry contents.

The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can change or withdraw it at any time using the “Manage consent” button below or in the corner of the page. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of analytics carried out before withdrawal.

The service provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework under the European Commission's adequacy decision. In Google Analytics, event and user data retention is set to 14 months. For user data, the period resets on new activity.

How long we retain data

Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to respond, clarify the matter raised and conclude discussions. If we continue working together, retention depends on the duration of the agreed project, legal documentation obligations and time limits for pursuing or defending possible claims. The scope of retained data should correspond to the purpose of retention. You can delete local drafts and your own project files independently as described above.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. Data portability applies to data processed by automated means on the basis of consent or a contract. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Send your request to gawrych.damian@gmail.com. As a rule, a response is provided within one month. If the GDPR permits an extension, you will be informed of the extension and its reason.

You may lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office. The Polish Personal Data Protection Office explains data subject rights. These rights are based on the General Data Protection Regulation — GDPR.
