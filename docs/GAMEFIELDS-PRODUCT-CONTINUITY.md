# Gamefields product continuity

This project evolves additively. New commercial, engineering or preview layers must not remove, replace or narrow previously established Gamefields business/product directions unless explicitly approved.

Preserved product areas include:
- Gamefields Studio / Builder and court visual design workflows
- renovation of existing courts and urban spaces
- new modular sports-field structures
- 3v3, 1v1/panna and additional sport expansion
- surfaces, graphics, equipment and branding systems
- Engineering / Production / Design Generator / Optimizer
- structural solver, Eurocode/stability, connections/foundations and fabrication exports
- Radar / map of renovation opportunities and place-based concepts
- event and activation offer
- objects / street furniture / associated infrastructure
- commercial quoting and proposal tools
- future art/print/wall-object directions and other previously established extensions

Implementation rule: prefer additive routes/modules and backward-compatible data contracts. Existing user journeys and business concepts should remain accessible unless there is an explicit migration plan.
